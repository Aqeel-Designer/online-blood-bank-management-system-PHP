# Online Blood Bank Management System

A web-based Online Blood Bank Management System developed to streamline blood donation and request management by connecting donors, recipients, hospitals, and administrators through a centralized platform.

The system aims to improve the accessibility and management of blood supplies by providing organized workflows for blood requests, donor management, hospital operations, and blood inventory tracking.

## Project Overview

The Online Blood Bank Management System is designed to simplify the process of finding available blood donors and managing blood-related requests.

It provides different functionalities based on user roles, allowing administrators, hospital representatives, donors, and recipients to interact with the system according to their responsibilities.

The project was initially developed as a university Final Year Project and is now being prepared for further development, including backend and database improvements.

## Key Features

- **Role-Based Access:** Separate functionalities for administrators, hospital representatives, donors, and recipients.
- **Donor Management:** Manage donor information, donation records, and eligibility details.
- **Blood Requests:** Allow recipients and hospitals to submit and manage blood requests.
- **Blood Inventory Management:** Maintain and monitor available blood stock.
- **Blood Search:** Search for blood availability based on blood group and location.
- **Request Management:** Review, approve, and manage blood requests.
- **Donation History:** Maintain donor donation records and next eligible donation dates.
- **Centralized Management:** Provide administrators with tools to manage users, hospitals, requests, and blood inventory.
